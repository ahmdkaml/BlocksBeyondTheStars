HOW TO PLAY

Click the game view once to capture your mouse (Esc releases it again).

ON FOOT
WASD move · Mouse look · Left click mine · Right click place · 1-9 hotbar · E use/interact · F attack · G loot · R repair · L lamp · M map · Tab menu · Enter chat

SPACE FLIGHT
WASD + mouse fly · 1-9 select ship system · Left click use system · E land/dock · L return to orbit · V change view · F board/leave your ship

SPACEWALK (EVA)
WASD + mouse float · Space/Ctrl up and down · float back to your ship and press E to get in

GETTING STARTED
Follow VEGA, your ship's AI: mine a few resources, craft your first tools, then launch into orbit and pick a new planet to explore. Build a base, grow food, tame creatures — the whole galaxy is block-built and yours to reshape.

MULTIPLAYER
In the shared arcade worlds you play with others: be friendly, build cool stuff, and use /report on players who misbehave.

Playing on a tablet or phone? Touch controls appear automatically.
